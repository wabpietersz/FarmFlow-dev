## Who usually does what

| Job | Daily | Weekly | Monthly |
|---|---|---|---|
| Farm manager | Today's check for each batch; health tasks due; attendance | Request stock; petty cash expenses | Close finished batches |
| Mill operator | Production runs; send feed to batches | Check raw material levels | Stock count |
| Store keeper | Receive deliveries; move stock to farms | Check expiring stock | Write off expired stock |
| Sales | Bookings; sales and lorry weights | Chase late payers | Send statements |
| Accounts | Receipts; supplier invoices and payments; cheques | Approvals; petty cash review | Payroll; EPF/ETF; profit & loss; cash flow; lock the month |
| Owner | Home; approvals | Owed to you; payables | Profit & loss; batch results |

## Words used in FarmFlow

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Batch | One flock of chicks placed in one house, from placement to sale |
| House | A poultry house on a farm (some screens say "cage") |
| Store | A place stock is kept: Main, Feed Mill, or a farm's own store |
| Lot | One delivery of one item, with its own cost and expiry date |
| Category | What money was for: Bird Sales, Day-old Chicks, Wages, Electricity… |
| Cost centre | Where money belongs: a farm, the Feed Mill, or Admin |
| Ledger | The list of every money movement |
| Receipt | Money received from a buyer |
| Bird-days | Number of live birds × number of days. Used to share farm costs fairly between batches |
| FCR | Feed conversion ratio: kg of feed used per kg of bird sold. Lower is better |
| EPEF | A single score for batch performance combining growth, survival and FCR. Higher is better |
| EPF / ETF | Staff provident fund (8% from the employee, 12% from the employer) and trust fund (3% from the employer) |
| Posted | Recorded in the ledger. Posted entries are reversed, never edited |
| Period lock | Closes a month so nothing in it can be changed |
