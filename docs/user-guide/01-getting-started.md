# 01 Getting started

For everyone.

## Signing in

1. Open the address you were given, on your phone or computer.
2. Enter your email and password.

**First time:** you'll get an email from FarmFlow with a link. Open it and choose a password. If no email comes, ask the admin: they can copy a link and send it to you by WhatsApp. The link works for one hour, and only the newest link works.

**Forgot it:** tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.

## Putting FarmFlow on your phone's home screen

- **Android (Chrome):** open the address, tap the **Install** prompt, or the three-dot menu → *Add to Home screen*.
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap the Share button → *Add to Home Screen*.

It then opens like an app, full screen.

## Finding your way around

**On a computer**, the bar across the top has: **Home · Farms · Feed mill · Stock · Sales · Money · People · Reports**. You only see the areas your role allows.

**On a phone**, the bar at the bottom has **Home · Farms · Sales · Money**, and **More** for everything else.

**Top right:**
- The **bell** shows things that need you.
- Your **initials** open a menu with **Approvals**, **Settings**, **Users & access** (if you're allowed), light/dark theme, and sign out.

## Home

| On Home | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Cash on hand | Total across all money accounts |
| Money in / out this month | From the ledger |
| Live birds | Across all growing batches |
| Profit, Owed to you, You owe suppliers, Waiting for approval | Owner and accounts only. Tap a tile for the detail |
| Batches growing | One card per batch: live birds, deaths, average weight, cost per kg |
| To do | Vaccinations due, days with no daily log, stock about to expire, invoices due, catches in the next 3 days, late payers, approvals waiting |

Start each day with the **To do** list.

If you're tied to one farm, you see only that farm's batches, sales, staff and reports.

## The bell

FarmFlow checks every 30 minutes and tells the right people about:

- vaccinations due or overdue
- stock expiring, expired or running low
- buyer cheques to deposit
- approvals waiting, and the answer to requests you raised
- catches tomorrow
- buyers who are late paying

Each thing is shown once. Tap it to go to the right screen.

## Working with no signal

Daily farm entry works offline.

1. Open FarmFlow while you have signal at least once that day, so it has your batches.
2. With no signal, a banner says you're offline. Carry on entering.
3. Your entries wait in a queue (the banner shows how many).
4. When signal returns they send by themselves. If one fails, the banner lets you retry or discard it.

Money, payroll and reports need signal.

**Pull down** on a phone screen to refresh it.

## When the app says "a new version is available"

Tap **Reload**. Nothing you've saved is lost.

## If something looks wrong

Note what screen you were on, what you did, what you expected and what happened. Take a screenshot. Send it to your FarmFlow contact.

## Good habits

- Enter things on the day they happen.
- Never share your sign-in. Every entry is recorded against the person signed in.
- If you entered something wrong and it has already been approved or paid, don't try to hide it: ask accounts to reverse it and enter it again.
